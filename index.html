  1. Long talks, short waits.

    While you talk, Dot sends what you have said in pieces. When you stop, only the last few seconds are left to transcribe.

  2. It types where your cursor is.

    Email, notes, code or chat, the words go into whatever you were working on. If you switch apps before it is done, Dot puts them on your clipboard instead.

  3. Pick your speech engine.

    Use Apple speech recognition on your Mac with macOS 26 or later, or add a key for OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Azure or another service.

  4. Your keys stay safe.

    API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain and sent only to the service you chose.

  5. Find what you said earlier.

    Your recent dictations stay on your Mac, so you can copy or paste one again.

Install.

Homebrewbrew install --cask anandlo/tap/dot
Terminalcurl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anandlo/dot/main/scripts/install-remote.sh | sh

Or download the disk image from Releases, or build from source. Dot is self-signed, so on first launch macOS asks once: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
